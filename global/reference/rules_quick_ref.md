### Spell Rules
- **Memorization**: 8 hours rest, then 1 hour study (wizard) or 1 hour prayer (priest)
- **Components**: V (verbal) + S (somatic) + M (material). Gagged = no V. Bound = no S.
- **Interruption**: Caster takes damage while casting → save vs. spell or lose the spell
- **Casting time**: Expressed in segments (1 segment = 6 seconds). 1 = fast, 9 = full round
- **Concentration**: Moving or being hit breaks concentration

## Combat

### Attack Roll
**To hit** = roll 1d20 ≥ (THAC0 − target AC). Roll 20 = always hit. Roll 1 = always miss.

Example: THAC0 18, target AC 5 → need 18 − 5 = 13 or better.

Negative AC: THAC0 18, target AC −2 → need 18 − (−2) = 20.

### Initiative
1. Each side rolls 1d10 (lower = goes first)
2. Individual initiative: 1d10 + weapon speed factor (lower = first)
3. Spells: casting time in segments added to initiative roll
4. Ties: simultaneous

## Death, Dying & Healing

### Hit Point States
| HP | State |
|----|-------|
| 1+ | Active |
| 0 | Unconscious; stable if no further damage |
| −1 to −9 | Dying — loses 1 HP/round until aided or dead |
| −10 | Dead |

**Binding wounds**: If tended within 1 round of reaching 0, stopped at 0 HP (stable, unconscious). Requires no magic.

### Natural Healing
- **Complete bed rest**: 3 HP/day (class variable; high-level fighters may recover more)
- **Light activity**: 1 HP/day
- **Adventuring**: No natural healing

### Resurrection
- Roll ≤ Resurrection Survival % (CON table) or the attempt fails
- Each resurrection attempt reduces CON by 1 permanently
- Limit: CON raises total possible; when exhausted, cannot be raised

### System Shock
Required when polymorphed, petrified, raised from dead, or subjected to massive magical transformation. Roll ≤ System Shock % (CON table) or die instantly.

## Surprise

- Each side rolls 1d10; 1 or 2 = surprised (typically)
- Surprised side loses their action for the surprise segment
- Surprised party cannot use shields (AC penalty)
- Thief/Ranger backstab only valid if attacker is unseen (surprise or stealth)

## Morale

For monsters/NPCs, when to check: first casualty, half the group down, leader falls, facing clearly superior force. Roll 2d10: if result > morale rating, they break and flee. (Use `reaction.py morale <rating>`.)
