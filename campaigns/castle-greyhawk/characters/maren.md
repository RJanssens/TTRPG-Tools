# Sister Maren

**Cleric**  Human  Female  Level 1

## Background

An acolyte of Pelor raised in a temple orphanage in Greyhawk City. Sister Maren took her vows young and has spent her novitiate tending the sick in the Artisan's Quarter. This is her first venture outside the city walls.
