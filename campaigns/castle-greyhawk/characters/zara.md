# Zara

**Magic-User**  Human  Female  Level 1

## Background

Apprenticed to a sage in the University District of Greyhawk City, Zara copied texts and ground components for six years before her master died insolvent. She took his spellbook and his debts and decided adventuring was the faster path to solvency.
