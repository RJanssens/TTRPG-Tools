# Peck

**Thief**  Halfling  Male  Level 1

## Background

Peck spent three years as a fence for the Thieves' Guild in Greyhawk's Thieves' Quarter before a deal went sour. He left in a hurry with a small purse and a large debt, and has been keeping his distance from the city ever since.
