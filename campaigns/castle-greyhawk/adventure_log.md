# Castle Greyhawk — Adventure Log

### Session 1 — The Dungeon of Misfit Monsters

**Day 1. Greyhawk City bazaar, then Greyhawk Dungeon Level 1.**

- Party formed around a notice board posting at the Nag's Head — never followed up. Instead encountered Culum McCreedy in the bazaar: a small red-haired boy (~10), known troublemaker, working a con with a tin cup and an "Orphan" sign.
- Peck saw through the performance immediately but gave him a copper. After questioning, Culum admitted his "poor ol' dad" is actually Zando the Great ("The Weasel"), a minor magician who attempted to convert Dungeon Level 1 into a monster zoo. The creatures escaped before opening day, Zando got locked up somewhere deeper in.
- Culum led the party through his house (Zando's shop) to a trapdoor entrance. Entrance has a ticket booth: Adults 3cp, Children under 12 free. Booth was empty.

**Areas explored:**
- **Area 1** — Entrance staircase, empty ticket booth, passage west.
- **Area 2** — Concession stand. Four tables, two beer taps, fireplace. Menu: Ale 10cp–2sp, Monsterburger 1sp, Full tour free. Exits: north (bear arrow), south passage (sea serpent arrow), south wooden door (bathrooms), west natural passage ("This way to tremendous monster of gigantic proportions"), east (exit).
- **Area 3** — Bathrooms. T-corridor, men's and women's doors. Both bathrooms clean, blue-tiled. Something large is living in the septic tank between them — a deep rumbling from the toilet opening. **Not investigated further.**
- **Area 10** — Porpoise cavern (sea serpent passage). Luminous green fungus walls. Stream with wooden bridge. Empty holding pool with dry moat. Sign: "Zando's Performing Porpoises — Showtimes 10, 12, 2, 4." Pool is empty. Two onward passages (stream, south). **Not explored further.**
- **Area 5** — Bear room (bear passage, north from Area 2). Centre of chamber cleared of all formations — exhibition space. Dense stalagmites/stalactites around perimeter, thick shadows on western wall. "EMPLOYEES ONLY" door on north wall. **Western shadows not investigated.**
- **Area 6** — Through Employees Only door from Area 5. Empty iron-bar cage against north wall, separated by dry moat. Plaque: "Extreme Danger — Do Not Feed Meat." Cage empty, bear bones on floor. Two exits: natural tunnel south (back toward Area 5), Employees Only door east.
- **Area 7** — Through Employees Only door east from Area 6. Natural chamber. Brownish-red stalagmites/stalactites, grey columns, grey flowstone. Thin clear stream winding east. Iron gate propped open against wall. **CURRENT LOCATION.**

**Combat pending:** A ropeless (roper without strands) is in the southeast corner of Area 7. Peck spotted it while moving parallel to the stream on a dry shelf. It has lunged and missed its opening attack. Peck is closest. Rest of party is in the stream ~8–10 feet away. Culum is against the far wall.

**Ropeless stats (module):** AC 0; Move 3"; HD 5; hp 15; #AT 1; Dmg 1d10; THACO 15; AL CE. Surprises on 1–5. Contains 3d6 pp and possibly 1d10 gems (35%).

**Party status:** All at full HP (Torven 9, Maren 7, Peck 5, Zara 4). No spells cast. No XP awarded. No coin. Session 1, Day 1.
- **TPK — Area 7, the Ropeless.** The creature (AC 0, 15 HP untouched) killed the entire party across 2 rounds: Peck first (round 1, 5 damage), then Torven (round 2, 3 damage — CLW heal failed to register in combat state), then Sister Maren (9 damage), then Zara (9 damage). Party never landed a single hit. Culum McCreedy is left alive in Area 7 with four corpses and the Ropeless.
